Nexus grew up on the Union world of Lucet, a planet devoted to "scholarship, craftsmanship, and community." The scholarship was great, the craftsmanship seemed to be mostly lip service, and the community meant a lot of extremely stifling social norms in the name of safety and public order. She had planned on following her mother into an academic career, but when some young representatives of the UN spoke to the school and started a "future mech pilots club," she was hooked. 

She loves piloting itself more than she's interested in the political reasons for deployment, though she'd argue (and possibly go so far as insubordination or desertion) if she believed that the fight was truly unjust. She's a hard worker and never complains about drills or maintenance, but she's not great with people. She was nearly expelled from her high school for her role in a fistfight in which another student was hospitalized. (She didn't start the fight, but she did finish it).

## Random Facts:

- When she's not working, she prefers to be dancing or playing contact sports.
- She's not above hacking data storage (like the school library) to get information she thinks she should have.
- She goes by Nexus, her callsign. Only those who have offical need-to-know are aware of her birth name.
