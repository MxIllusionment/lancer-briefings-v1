Tom Right is the kind of pilot who makes an entrance whether it’s called for or not—flashy, cocky, and utterly convinced he’s the most interesting person in any room (or dropship). Despite an academic career spent creatively skirting effort and a near-death reactor incident that should have humbled him, he’s still all smiles, all charm, and all-in on his next stunt. His mech, *Silver Stallion*, is as dramatic and stylish as he is.

## Random Facts:

- Has a soft spot for stray animals. He once delayed a launch to rescue a feral cat from the hangar.
- Creates a custom "nose art" for his mech every time he has a new frame printed. He always paints it on himself.
- Once flirted with a customs official mid-arrest; it didn’t work, but he still insists he “softened the charges.”
