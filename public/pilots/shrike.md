Shen Torral was born and raised on the rough streets of Choth 42-L, a resource heavy moon in the densely inhabited Choth system, just outside of Union space where human life means very little to the system overlords. Fleeing the press gangs sweeping up citizens "privileged" to replace the latest swathe of dying miners, Shen made their way into Union space, becoming a petty criminal until caught and given the offer of deportation back to the Choth system or joining the Union military. Luckily for Shen, they thrived as a cadet, quickly rising to squad leader for a Lancer team.

## Random Facts:

- Shen loves to build their own legend. See the academy incident where they "disappeared" for ten days.
- The only thing citizens of 42-L had for food was a bitter, grainy nutri-paste, and Union military MRE's remind Shen way too much of that gunk, so they always sneak candy into their rations for deployments
- Shen has a carefully guarded secret: They really love rom-com holo-films. They won't admit this under penalty of death
