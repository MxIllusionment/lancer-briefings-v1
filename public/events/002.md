# Nov Elysia // 7.29.5016u 0600
Vestan Sovereignty forces have launched an attack on **Nov Elysia** during negotations. In transit, our drop pod was attacked and forced to crash land in **Kirmani Plaza**. We have been informed that Captain Farris and Ambassador Bannerjee are MIA. 

✔ **Staff Sergeant Garcia**'s dropship went down near **Downtown**. The team has successfully located and rescued Garcia and he is en route to FOB Saber.

✔ Missile fire from a **Train Depot** shot down a cargo ship. The team has eliminated the mech responsible, opening the way for support shuttles. The pilot has been identified as **Executor Mlada Jacquinot**, an officer of the VSAF.

✔ **Captain Farris** has been rescued from a VSAF prison transport in the **Harbor District**. With the Harbor secure, the main VSAF supply lines have been cut off.

✔ The jamming signal in the **Residential District** has been neutralized, allowing for large-scale coordination of Union and LSA forces. The VSAF forces are being steadily pushed out of the city. 

✔ **Ambassador Bannerjee** was located in the **Capitol District** and successfully exfiltrated without conflict. They have been secured at FOB Saber.

Captain Farris has confirmed she was captured by **Colonel Sorvan Kiros**, a VSAF officer known for extreme methods. He is piloting an advanced mech and accompanied by a group of elite mech pilots referred to as "the Furies". In a last ditch attempt at disrupting Union activities, Kiros and his followers have launched a full on assault against FOB Saber. The fate of Cressidium lies in the balance...
