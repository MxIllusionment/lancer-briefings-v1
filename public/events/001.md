# UNS-CV Rio Grande // 7.17.5016u
**Cressidium**: a once-forgotten planet settled during the final days of Union’s Second Committee. As revolution erupted across Union space, fleeing SecComm loyalists made Cressidium their hidden refuge, severing contact with the galaxy. Over the centuries, the planet endured factional strife and transformation, eventually developing into a complex society of independent nations shaped by both legacy and rebellion.

Now, five centuries later, a Third Committee survey team has rediscovered the world. Diplomatic negotiations are underway, led by **Union Ambassador Nilan Bannerjee**, focusing on two of Cressidium’s major powers: the Union-friendly **Leandric States Alliance** and the cautious, isolationist **Vestan Sovereignty**. Tensions run high as Union’s presence stirs long-standing rivalries, with talks centered in the LSA city of Nov Elysia.

Stationed in orbit aboard the **UNS-CV Rio Grande**, our squad is part of the support detail for this diplomatic mission.
